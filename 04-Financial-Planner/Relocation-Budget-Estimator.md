## 💡 Financial Optimization Recommendations

1. **Zero Brokerage Strategy**: Use **NoBroker Pune Relax/Super Relax Plan** (~₹3,499) to unlock unlimited direct owner contacts and dedicated relationship managers, saving up to ₹40,000 in brokerage fees.
2. **Coworking Pre-Booking Negotiation**: For 4 desks, never accept the website rack rate. Request a curated quote through **myHQ** or **CoFynd** to secure an immediate 10% to 20% discount on 3-month or 6-month commitments.
3. **GST Input Tax Credit**: Ensure your coworking contract is registered under your business GSTIN to reclaim the 18% GST on office workspace invoices.
