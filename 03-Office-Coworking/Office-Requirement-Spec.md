# 4-Flex Desk Workspace Technical & Operational Specification

This specification outlines the minimum operational, technical, and commercial criteria required for the team's office space in Pune.

---

## 1. Physical Seating & Workspace Layout

- **Capacity**: 4 Flex Desks (hot desks / collaborative open floor) with option to upgrade to Dedicated Desks.
- **Ergonomics**: High-back mesh chairs with lumbar support and standard height workstations (minimum 1000mm x 600mm desk surface per person).
- **Power Sockets**: At least 2 dedicated 6A/16A universal power outlets per seat with surge protection.
- **Quiet Zones & Phone Booths**: Minimum 2 acoustic single-person phone booths available on the floor for confidential client calls.

---

## 2. IT Infrastructure & Network SLAs

- **Internet Speed**: Minimum 100 Mbps dedicated symmetric bandwidth per team member.
- **Redundancy**: Dual ISP leased lines (e.g., Tata Communications + Bharti Airtel) configured with automated BGP failover.
- **Uptime SLA**: Minimum **99.5% uptime** guarantee during business operating hours.
- **Power Redundancy**: 100% online UPS backup with zero-switchover latency + secondary diesel generator (DG) backup.

---

## 3. Meeting Rooms & Collaboration

- **Monthly Credits**: Minimum **6 to 8 hours** of bookable meeting room credits per month for 4–6 person conference rooms.
- **AV Equipment**: 4K Presentation display, HDMI/Type-C wireless projection, and polycom/conference microphone setups in meeting rooms.
- **Whiteboards**: Mobile magnetic whiteboards accessible in collaborative breakout zones.

---

## 4. Facility & Operational Amenities

- **Access Hours**: 24/7 biometric / RFID card access for team members.
- **Beverages & Pantry**: Unlimited filtered water, freshly brewed micro-roasted coffee, and specialty teas.
- **Printing / Scanning**: Minimum 100 black-and-white / 20 color prints per month included.
- **Parking Allocation**: Dedicated two-wheeler parking slots and reserved / visitor car parking slots.

---

## 5. Commercial Terms Checklist

- **Billing Model**: Monthly recurring invoice with GST input tax credit availability.
- **Security Deposit**: Maximum **1 to 2 months** desk rental fee (refundable within 30 days of exit).
- **Notice Period**: Standard 30-day notice period without restrictive long-term lock-in clauses.
