# Coworking & Flexible Office Comparison (4-Flex / Dedicated Desks)

This guide benchmarks leading coworking operators across Baner/Balewadi, Wakad/Hinjewadi, and Kharadi for a **4-person team**, complete with **direct booking URLs**, day pass links, verified pricing, and infrastructure details.

## 1. West Pune: Baner & Balewadi Hub

### A. WeWork – Eleven West (Veerbhadra Nagar, Baner)
- **Direct Booking Link**: [https://wework.co.in/workspace/pune/eleven-west-baner](https://wework.co.in/workspace/pune/eleven-west-baner)
- **Day Pass Booking**: [https://wework.co.in/pass](https://wework.co.in/pass) (₹850 – ₹950 / person / day)
- **Vibe & Atmosphere**: Ultra-premium global standard, high-ceiling architectural lounges, buzzing startup community, wellness rooms.
- **Internet SLA**: Dual-homed leased lines (1 Gbps symmetric) with automatic BGP failover and 99.9% uptime SLA.
- **Pricing for 4 People**:
  - *Flex Desks (4 Seats)*: **₹38,000 / mo** + 18% GST (₹6,840) = **₹44,840 / month total** (₹9,500/seat).
  - *Dedicated Desks (4 Seats)*: **₹48,000 / mo** + 18% GST (₹8,640) = **₹56,640 / month total** (₹12,000/seat).
- **Meeting Room Credits**: 8 credits/month included.
- **Proximity**: 1.0–2.5 km from Baner & Balewadi societies (5–8 mins).
