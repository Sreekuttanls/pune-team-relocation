# Coworking & Flexible Office Comparison (4-Flex / Dedicated Desks)

This guide benchmarks the leading coworking operators in Pune across Baner/Balewadi, Wakad/Hinjewadi, and Kharadi for a **4-person team**.

---

## 1. West Pune: Baner & Balewadi Hub

### A. WeWork – Eleven West (Veerbhadra Nagar, Baner)
- **Vibe & Atmosphere**: Ultra-premium global standard, high-ceiling architectural lounges, buzzing startup community, podcast & wellness rooms.
- **Connectivity & Internet**: Dual-homed leased lines (1 Gbps symmetric) with automatic BGP failover and 99.9% uptime SLA.
- **Pricing Breakdown**:
  - *Flex / Hot Desk (4 Seats)*: **₹34,000 – ₹42,000 / month** (₹8,500 – ₹10,500/seat)
  - *Dedicated Desks (4 Seats)*: **₹48,000 – ₹56,000 / month** (₹12,000 – ₹14,000/seat)
  - *Day Pass*: ₹850 – ₹950 / person / day
- **Meeting Room Allocation**: 8–12 monthly meeting room credits across the team.
- **Distance from Residences**: 1.0–2.5 km from Baner & Balewadi societies (5–8 mins).

### B. Awfis – The Kode (Baner-Pashan Link Road) & Sterling Tower (Baner)
- **Vibe & Atmosphere**: Functional, sleek, tech-focused business center with modern meeting pods and quiet call booths.
- **Pricing Breakdown**:
  - *Flex Desk (4 Seats)*: **₹30,000 – ₹34,000 / month** (₹7,500 – ₹8,500/seat)
  - *Dedicated Desks (4 Seats)*: **₹38,000 / month** (₹9,500/seat)
  - *Day Pass*: ₹500 – ₹650 / person / day
- **Meeting Room Allocation**: 8 meeting room hours included monthly.
- **Distance from Residences**: 0.5–1.5 km from Mont Vert/Rohan Leher (2–4 mins walk/drive).

### C. IndiQube ATP (Amar Tech Park, Balewadi)
- **Vibe & Atmosphere**: Enterprise-grade commercial tech park ambiance, dedicated cafeteria, recreation and gaming zones.
- **Pricing Breakdown**:
  - *Flex Desk (4 Seats)*: **₹26,000 – ₹30,000 / month** (₹6,500 – ₹7,500/seat)
  - *Dedicated Desks (4 Seats)*: **₹30,000 – ₹36,000 / month** (₹7,500 – ₹9,000/seat)
  - *Day Pass*: ₹450 – ₹600 / person / day
- **Distance from Residences**: 0.8–1.5 km from Balewadi High Street / Aditya Comfort Zone (3–5 mins).

### D. Workflo by OYO (Icon Tower, Baner)
- **Vibe & Atmosphere**: High-efficiency, budget-optimized open coworking floor.
- **Pricing Breakdown**:
  - *Flex Desk (4 Seats)*: **₹22,000 – ₹26,000 / month** (₹5,500 – ₹6,500/seat)
  - *Dedicated Desks (4 Seats)*: **₹28,000 – ₹32,000 / month** (₹7,000 – ₹8,000/seat)
  - *Day Pass*: ₹450 – ₹550 / person / day
- **Distance from Residences**: 1.5–2.5 km from Baner societies.

## 📊 Summary Comparison: Budget vs Value Tiers (4 Pax)

1. **Ultra-Premium Tier** (*WeWork Eleven West Baner / WeWork WTC Kharadi*):
   - Monthly Cost: **₹34,000 – ₹44,000**
   - Highlights: World-class brand image, networking, premium cafe amenities, soundproof call booths.
2. **Standard Enterprise Tier** (*Awfis / IndiQube / Smartworks*):
   - Monthly Cost: **₹26,000 – ₹38,000**
   - Highlights: Professional grade-A office setups, meeting room credits, ergonomic chairs, 100% power backup.
3. **High-Value Startup Tier** (*Trios Wakad / Bootstart / Workflo*):
   - Monthly Cost: **₹18,000 – ₹26,000**
   - Highlights: Maximum cost savings, flexible contracts, essential 24/7 internet and power redundancy.
