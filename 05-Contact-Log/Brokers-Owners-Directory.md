## 3. Communication Templates for Landlords & Coworking Managers

### A. WhatsApp / Message Template for Apartment Landlords:
```text
Hi [Owner Name],
I came across your 3 BHK listing at [Society Name, e.g., Blue Ridge / Aditya Comfort Zone] on [NoBroker/Housing]. 
We are a professional tech team relocating to Pune. We are looking for a clean, well-maintained flat with immediate/near-term move-in.
Could you please confirm:
1. Is the flat currently available for visit?
2. Does the quoted rent of ₹[Amount] include society maintenance?
3. Is power backup available for internal points?
4. What is the expected security deposit?
Looking forward to scheduling a quick visit this week. Thank you!
```

### B. Email / Inquiry Template for Coworking Managers (4 Desks):
```text
Subject: Inquiry: 4-Flex / Dedicated Desks Quotation for Tech Team at [Coworking Location]

Hi Team [Coworking Brand, e.g. Awfis / WeWork / IndiQube],

We are a software team relocating to Pune and looking to onboard 4 flex/dedicated desks starting from [Target Move-in Date].

Could you please share:
1. Best monthly package for 4 flex/dedicated seats (including GST).
2. Number of complimentary meeting room hours and printing credits included.
3. Security deposit requirement and notice period.
4. Internet SLA / dual ISP redundancy details.
5. Availability of a trial day pass for our team to test the workspace.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name / Team Lead]
```
