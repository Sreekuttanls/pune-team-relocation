# Furnishing Strategy Guide: Turnkey Furnished vs Semi-Furnished + Rental

When searching for a 3 BHK under ₹40,000 in Pune, teams generally choose between two primary paths.

---

## Strategy A: Direct Turnkey Fully-Furnished 3 BHK

### Ideal Localities:
- **Hinjewadi & Marunji** (*Kolte Patil Life Republic, Megapolis, Paranjape Blue Ridge*)
- **Wakad** (*Rohan Tarang, Costa Rica, Signature Park*)
- **Mahalunge** (*Godrej Hillside, VTP Alpine*)

### Financials:
- **Monthly Rent**: ₹33,000 – ₹40,000 (Inclusive of all furniture & appliances).
- **Security Deposit**: 2 – 3 months (₹70,000 – ₹1,20,000).
- **Setup Time**: Immediate move-in (0 days setup).

### Pros:
- Zero upfront furniture procurement hassle.
- Landlord maintains major existing fittings.
- Single payment invoice each month.

### Cons:
- Limited aesthetic control (furniture style depends on landlord's taste).
- Higher security deposit deduction risk if landlord claims furniture wear-and-tear upon move-out.

---

## Strategy B: High-Spec Semi-Furnished 3 BHK + Subscription Furniture

### How it Works:
In Pune, high-end semi-furnished flats come with:
- Complete modular kitchen with chimney & cabinets
- Built-in wardrobes in all 3 bedrooms
- Basic electrical fittings (ceiling fans, tube lights, exhaust fans, geysers)
- Dedicated car parking

You then subscribe to modern furniture & appliances through **Furlenco**, **Rentomojo**, or **Cityfurnish**.

### Subscription Package Breakdown for a 3-Bedroom Team Setup:

| Item Package | Quantity | Approx. Monthly Subscription |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Queen Bed + Mattress + Pillows** | 3 Units | ₹1,800 – ₹2,400 |
| **3-Seater / L-Shaped Living Sofa + Center Table** | 1 Set | ₹700 – ₹1,000 |
| **Double-Door Refrigerator (240L+)** | 1 Unit | ₹600 – ₹800 |
| **Fully Automatic Washing Machine (6.5kg+)** | 1 Unit | ₹550 – ₹750 |
| **43" 4K Smart TV** | 1 Unit | ₹650 – ₹900 |
| **Dining Table (4-Seater)** | 1 Set | ₹400 – ₹600 |
| **Total Monthly Furniture Subscription** | — | **~₹4,700 – ₹6,450 / month** |

### Financial Comparison:
- Semi-Furnished Rent: **₹30,000 – ₹34,000**
- Furniture Subscription: **₹5,000**
- **Total Effective Monthly Outlay**: **₹35,000 – ₹39,000 / month**

### Pros:
- Unlock brand-new, modern Scandinavian aesthetic furniture.
- Free relocation, free deep cleaning, and free maintenance by the rental provider.
- Broader selection of premium gated societies (like Mont Vert, VJ Yashwin, Kunal Iconia, Aditya Comfort Zone).
