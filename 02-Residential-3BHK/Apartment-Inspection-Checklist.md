# 3 BHK Apartment Inspection & Verification Checklist

Use this checklist during physical or virtual property inspections in Pune before signing the rental agreement.

---

## 1. Water Supply & Utility Verification (Crucial in Pune)

- [ ] **Water Source**: Is it direct PMC / PCMC pipeline water, 24/7 society borewell + STP, or reliant on daily private water tankers?
- [ ] **Water Pressure & Timings**: Test showers, kitchen taps, and all bathroom fittings. Verify if water is available 24/7 or only during fixed morning/evening hours.
- [ ] **Water Metering**: Is water included in the society maintenance or billed separately per flat?
- [ ] **Geysers**: Check if all 3 bathrooms have functional electric/solar geysers installed.

---

## 2. Power Backup & Electrical Infrastructure

- [ ] **Internal Backup**: Does the society's Diesel Generator (DG) power light/fan points inside the apartment during power cuts, or only common areas and elevators?
- [ ] **Inverter Provision**: If DG backup is only for common areas, is there a dedicated inverter unit or dedicated inverter wiring pre-installed?
- [ ] **AC Points & High-Load Sockets**: Verify that all 3 bedrooms and the living room have functional 15A/16A sockets and dedicated AC outdoor unit mountings.
- [ ] **Broadband Fiber Feasibility**: Check which fiber ISPs are operational in the society (e.g., Tata Play Fiber, Airtel Xstream, JioFiber) and verify port availability.

---

## 3. Furnishing & Interior Condition Check

- [ ] **Kitchen Modular Fittings**: Check soft-close drawers, chimney suction, piped gas (MNGL connection) vs cylinder slot, and sink drainage.
- [ ] **Wardrobes & Storage**: Inspect built-in wardrobes in all 3 bedrooms for dampness, sliding track smoothness, and locks.
- [ ] **Balconies & Safety Grills**: Ensure all balconies and dry balconies have safety grills and pigeon netting installed.
- [ ] **Appliance Functionality (For Fully Furnished)**: Test Refrigerator, Automatic Washing Machine, Microwave/Oven, Smart TV, and Air Conditioners.

---

## 4. Society Rules & Community Policies

- [ ] **Tenant Category NOC**: Check if the society managing committee has restrictions on bachelor/team sharing (some societies require formal interview or higher non-refundable move-in charges).
- [ ] **Move-in / Move-out Charges**: Clarify one-time society shifting charges (typically ₹1,000 – ₹3,000 in Pune).
- [ ] **Parking Slots**: Verify allocated reserved covered four-wheeler (car) parking slot number and two-wheeler parking spots.
- [ ] **Clubhouse & Gym Access**: Confirm that tenants receive full biometric/RFID access cards to the gym, swimming pool, and sports courts without extra tenant fees.

---

## 5. Lease & Financial Terms

- [ ] **Rent Breakdown**: Explicitly agree in writing whether the quoted rent **includes society maintenance** (₹2,500 – ₹5,500/mo).
- [ ] **Security Deposit**: Negotiate to the Pune standard of **2 to 3 months of rent** (avoid paying 5–6 months).
- [ ] **Lock-in Period & Notice Period**: Standard is 1-month notice period after a 6-month lock-in (or 11-month agreement).
- [ ] **Painting & Maintenance Deductions**: Standard deduction upon vacating is 1 month's rent for painting or actual painter bills; ensure this is documented clearly in the registered rent agreement.
- [ ] **Registered Agreement & Police Verification**: Landlord and tenant must complete mandatory Pune Police biometric registration.
