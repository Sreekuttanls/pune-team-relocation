# Locality Guide: Baner & Balewadi (West Pune)

## 1. Overview & Neighborhood Dynamics
The **Baner–Balewadi corridor** is widely considered the prime residential and commercial hotspot for startup founders, tech teams, and product companies in Pune. It sits strategically between central Pune (Shivajinagar/Aundh) and the Hinjewadi IT Park, right off the Mumbai–Pune Expressway.

- **Vibe**: Cosmopolitan, high-energy, vibrant café culture, high concentration of startups and tech executives.
- **Social Infrastructure**: Balewadi High Street (gourmet restaurants, microbreweries, specialty cafes, gyms), Jupiter Hospital, Balewadi Sports Complex.
- **Commute**: 10–15 minutes to Hinjewadi Phase 1, 20 minutes to Shivajinagar.
