# Locality Guide: Kharadi, Viman Nagar & East Pune

## 1. Overview & Neighborhood Dynamics
The **East Pune Tech Corridor** centers around **Kharadi** (EON IT Park, World Trade Center Pune, Zensar Knowledge Park) and extends to **Viman Nagar** (near Pune International Airport and Phoenix Marketcity).

- **Vibe**: Fast-growing IT powerhouse, modern commercial towers, broad concrete roads, close to the airport.
- **Social Infrastructure**: Phoenix Marketcity Mall, Amanora & Seasons Mall (nearby Hadapsar), Columbia Asia / Manipal Hospital.
- **Commute**: 5–10 minutes within Kharadi; 15–20 minutes to Viman Nagar and Koregaon Park.

---

## 2. 3 BHK Housing Market Analysis (< ₹40,000 Budget)

Kharadi has seen rapid construction of large gated communities. While ultra-luxe projects (Marvel, Riverdale Grand) exceed ₹55k, many prime societies offer modern 3 BHKs within ₹35,000 – ₹40,000.

### Top Residential Societies:

1. **VJ Yashwin Enchante / Supernova (Upper Kharadi)**:
   - *Rent Range*: **₹32,000 – ₹38,000 (Semi-Furnished)**, **₹40,000 – ₹44,000 (Furnished)**.
   - *Highlights*: Contemporary designs, clubhouse, swimming pool, sports courts, 100% DG backup.
2. **Godrej Parkridge / Infinity / Sky Greens (Manjari-Kharadi)**:
   - *Rent Range*: **₹30,000 – ₹36,000 (Semi-Furnished)**, **₹38,000 – ₹44,000 (Furnished)**.
   - *Highlights*: Green open riverfront spaces, modern towers, Olympic-size pool, extensive club facilities.
3. **Kohinoor Zen Estate / Zen Elite (Kharadi Riverfront)**:
   - *Rent Range*: **₹36,000 – ₹42,000**.
   - *Highlights*: Walking distance to Zensar & EON IT Park, 3-tier security, modern floor plans.
4. **Bramha Suncity (Wadgaon Sheri / Kalyani Nagar Border)**:
   - *Rent Range*: **₹38,000 – ₹45,000**.
   - *Highlights*: 25-acre township, central artificial lake, high carpet areas (1400+ sq.ft).
