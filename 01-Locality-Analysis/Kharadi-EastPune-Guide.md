# Locality Guide: Kharadi, Viman Nagar & East Pune

## 1. Overview & Neighborhood Dynamics
The **East Pune Tech Corridor** centers around **Kharadi** (EON IT Park, World Trade Center Pune, Zensar Knowledge Park) and extends to **Viman Nagar** (near Pune International Airport and Phoenix Marketcity).

- **Vibe**: Fast-growing IT powerhouse, modern commercial towers, broad concrete roads, close to the airport.
- **Social Infrastructure**: Phoenix Marketcity Mall, Amanora & Seasons Mall (nearby Hadapsar), Columbia Asia / Manipal Hospital.
- **Commute**: 5–10 minutes within Kharadi; 15–20 minutes to Viman Nagar and Koregaon Park.
